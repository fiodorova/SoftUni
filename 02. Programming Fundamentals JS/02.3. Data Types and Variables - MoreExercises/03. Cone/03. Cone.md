**3.	Cone**

Write a **function** to calculate a cone’s **volume** and **total surface area** by given height and radius of the base. </br> 
The **input** comes as two number arguments. The first element is the cone’s **radius** and the second is its **height**. </br> 
The **output** should be printed to the console on a **new line** for every result. The result should be formatted to the **fourth decimal point**.
