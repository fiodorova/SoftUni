**6.	\*Extract Emails**

Write a function to **extract all email addresses from a given text.** The text comes at the only input line. Print the emails on the console, each at a separate line. Emails are considered to be in format **user>@<host>**, where: </br>
&emsp;•	**<user>** is a sequence of **letters** and **digits**, where **'.', '-'** and **'_'** can appear between them. </br>
&emsp;&emsp;o	Examples of valid users: **"stephan", "mike03", "s.johnson", "st_steward", "softuni-bulgaria", "12345"**. </br>
&emsp;&emsp;o	Examples of invalid users: **''--123", ".....", "nakov_-", "_steve", ".info"**.  </br>
&emsp;•	**<host>** is a sequence of at least two words, separated by dots '.'. Each word is a sequence of letters and can have hyphens '-' between the letters. </br>
&emsp;&emsp;o	Examples of hosts: **"softuni.bg", "software-university.com", "intoprogramming.info", "mail.softuni.org"**.  </br>
&emsp;&emsp;o	Examples of invalid hosts: **"helloworld", ".unknown.soft.", "invalid-host-", "invalid-"**. 


•	Examples of **valid emails:** 
<pre>
info\@softuni-bulgaria.org 
kiki@hotmail.co.uk 
no-reply@github.com 
s.peterson@mail.uu.net 
info-bg@software-university.software.academy
</pre>
•	Examples of **invalid emails:** 
<pre>
--123@gmail.com
…@mail.bg 
.info@info.info
_steve@yahoo.cn 
mike@helloworld 
mike@.unknown.soft. 
s.johnson@invalid-
  </pre>
