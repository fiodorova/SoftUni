**9.	\*Dungeonest Dark**
   
&emsp; *As a young adventurer, you seek gold and glory in the darkest dungeons there are.*

You have **initial health 100** and **initial coins 0**. You will be given **a string**, representing the **dungeon's rooms**. Each room is separated with **'|'** (vertical bar): **"room1|room2|room3…"**

Each room contains - an **item** or a **monster**; and a number. They are separated by a single space.
 **("item/monster number")**. </br>
&emsp; •	If the first part is **"potion"**: </br>
&emsp; &emsp; o	You are healed with the number in the second part. However, your health **cannot exceed** your **initial health (100)**. </br>
&emsp; &emsp; o	Print: **`You healed for {healing-number} hp.`** </br>
&emsp; &emsp; o	After that, print your current health: **`Current health: {number} hp.`** </br>
&emsp; •	If the first part is **"chest"**: </br>
&emsp; &emsp; o	You have found some coins, the number in the second part. </br>
&emsp; &emsp; o	Print: **`You found {coins} coins.`** </br>
&emsp; •	In any other case, you are facing a monster, you are going to fight. </br>
The second part of the room contains the attack of the monster, and the first the monster's name. You should remove the monster's attack from your health.  </br>
&emsp; &emsp; o	If you are not dead **(health > 0)** you have slain the monster, and you should print: </br>
                                   **`You slayed {monster-name}.`** </br>
&emsp; &emsp; o	If you have died, print: **`You died! Killed by {monster-name}.`** and your quest is over. </br>
Print the best room you`ve to manage to reach: **`Best room: {room}`**. </br>
&emsp; •	If you managed to go through all the rooms in the dungeon, print on the next three lines: </br>
&emsp; **"You've made it!"** </br>
&emsp; **"Coins: {coins}** </br>
&emsp;  **"Health: {health}"** </br>

**Input** </br>
You receive an array with one element- string, representing the dungeon's rooms, separated with **'|'** (vertical bar): **["room1|room2|room3…"]**.

**Output** </br>
Print the corresponding messages, described above.
