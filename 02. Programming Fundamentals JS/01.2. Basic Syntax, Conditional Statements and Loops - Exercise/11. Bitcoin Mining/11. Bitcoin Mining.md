**11.	* Bitcoin "Mining"**
    
Write a JavaScript program that calculates the **total amount** of **bitcoins** you purchased with the gold you mined during your **shift** at the mine. Your shift consists of a certain number of days where you mine an amount of **gold** in **grams**. Your program will receive an **array with the amount of gold** you mined **each day**, where the **first day** of your **shift** is the **first index of the array**. Also, someone was stealing **every third day** from the start of your shift **30%** from the mined **gold** for **this day**. You need to check, which day you have enough money to buy your **first bitcoin**. For the different exchanges use these **prices**: </br>
&emsp; **1 Bitcoin**&emsp; 	11949.16 lv. </br>
&emsp; **1 g of gold**	67.51 lv.

**Input** </br>
You will receive an array of **numbers**, representing your **shift** at the mine.

**Output** </br>
Print on the **console these lines in the following formats**: </br>
&emsp; ·	**First-line** prints the **total amount** of bought **bitcoins**: </br>
&emsp; &emsp;  **\`Bought bitcoins: {count}\`** </br>
&emsp; ·	**Second-line** prints **which day** you **bought** your **first bitcoin**: </br>
&emsp; &emsp;  **\`Day of the first purchased bitcoin: {day}\`** </br>
&emsp; In case you **did not purchase any bitcoins**, do not print the second line. </br>
&emsp; ·	**Third-line** prints the **amount** of **money** that’s left after the bitcoin purchases **rounded by the second digit** after the decimal point: </br>
 &emsp; &emsp;  **\`Left money: {money} lv.\`**
  
**Constraints** </br>
&emsp; ·	The **input** array may contain up to **1,000** elements </br>
&emsp; ·	The numbers in the array are in the range **[0.01..5,000.00] inclusive** </br>
&emsp; ·	Allowed time/memory: 100ms/16MB
