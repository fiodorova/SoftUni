**8. Perfect Number**

Write a function that receives a **number** and checks if that number is perfect or NOT.

A perfect number is a **positive** integer that is equal to the **sum** of its **proper positive divisors**. That is the sum of its positive **divisors** excluding the number itself (also known as its **aliquot sum**).

**Output** </br>
&emsp; •	If the number is perfect, print: **"We have a perfect number!"** </br>
&emsp; •	Otherwise, print: **"It's not so perfect."**

**Hint** </br>
Equivalently, a perfect number is a number that is **half the sum** of all of its positive divisors (including itself) => 6 is a perfect number because it is the sum of 1 + 2 + 3 (all of which are divided without residue). </br>
