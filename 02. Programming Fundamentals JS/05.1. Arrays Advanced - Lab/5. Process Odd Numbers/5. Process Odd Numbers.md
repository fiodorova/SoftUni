**5.	Process Odd Numbers**

You are given an **array of numbers**. Write a function that prints the elements at **odd positions** from the array, **doubled** and in **reverse** order.

The **input** comes as an array of number elements.

The **output** is printed on the console on a single line, separated by space.

**Hints** </br>
&emsp; •	Counting in arrays starts from 0 </br>
&emsp; •	For example –we receive 10, 15, 20, 25 </br>
&emsp; •	The elements at odd positions are 15 (index 1) and 25 (index 3) </br>
&emsp; •	We need to take these two elements and multiply them * 2 </br>
&emsp; •	Finally, we print them on the console in reversed order
